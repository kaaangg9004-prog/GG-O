# Assets

## Original concept graphics

`tote.svg`, `tint.svg`, `keyring.svg`, `cap.svg`, `pouch.svg` were drawn for this development version. They are concept illustrations, not photographs of real inventory. The website wordmark and graffiti emblem were prepared with image generation/editing from the five logo reference images the user attached in this conversation. They are reference-based web assets, not claimed as pixel-exact extractions. The compact wordmark is used in the header/footer and app icons; the black/white/neon graffiti emblem is used in the brand panel. Selectable product color variants remain editable SVG web artwork.

## Language assets

Country flags are bundled SVGs from https://flagcdn.com/kr.svg, https://flagcdn.com/vn.svg and https://flagcdn.com/gb.svg. They are served locally. Vietnamese and English lettering variants of the original product SVG artwork are generated by `scripts/localize-assets.ts`; no remote translation service is used by the website.

`event-placeholder.svg` is a locally drawn calendar icon used until an event cover is selected. Event photo presets reuse the bundled cafe, friends and concert sample photos listed above.

The existing product concept illustrations now illustrate opinion polls. They are not offers for sale. The supplied GG-O wordmark is also used in the branded sign-in modal. No new external image or font service is introduced.
